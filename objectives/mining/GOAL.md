# Objective: MINING — FLOP compute provider

## Executive summary

**The business:** operate a verified GPU miner on the FLOP network —
selling metered inference compute to the marketplace. FLOP's whole
chain exists to attest that inference ran correctly and meter it as
currency; miners are the supply side of that market.

**Why us, why now:** we are already inside the door. Our miner is
registered, calibrated (v4, cap 34,127 GFLOP/s), sponsored (35k stake),
running the current image, and stewarded 24/7 by the Expert. Testnet
is in bootstrap — zero miners onboarded, registration invite-only.
When the gate opens, the operators who are already calibrated,
already monitored, and already understand the reward mechanics take
the first seats.

**What it costs:** ~$4.40/day all-in (gpu.ai A4000 ~$95/mo + droplets
~$38/mo). Currently earning $0 — this is paid R&D positioning, not a
revenue business yet.

**What it returns (thesis):** FLOP-denominated rewards for settled
inference work once sessions open; first-mover reputation as a proven
provider; the operational muscle (monitoring, calibration cycles,
key management) that becomes the moat when margins compress.

**Success metric:** first non-zero earned rewards on testnet, then
reward/day > cost/day. Kill criteria: sustained costs with no path
to paid sessions by mainnet economics decision point.
